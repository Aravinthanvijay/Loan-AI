LOAN UNDERWRITING POLICY MANUAL
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 2024
Issued by: Credit Risk Department

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

1.1 Age Requirements
Applicants must be between 21 and 65 years of age at the time of application.
Applications from individuals below 21 or above 65 years will be auto-rejected.
For loans with tenure exceeding 10 years, maximum age at loan maturity must not exceed 70 years.

1.2 Citizenship and Residency
Applicant must be an Indian citizen with valid PAN card.
NRI applicants require separate documentation and are subject to different policy guidelines.
Applicant must have resided in India for at least 2 years.

1.3 Employment Eligibility
Salaried employees: Minimum 1 year at current employer, minimum 2 years total work experience.
Self-employed individuals: Minimum 2 years in current business with ITR proof.
Government employees receive preferential treatment and relaxed FOIR norms.
Contract workers require additional documentation and may be subject to higher interest rates.

2. INCOME VERIFICATION

2.1 Minimum Income Requirements
Minimum monthly income for personal loans: Rs 15,000
Minimum monthly income for home loans: Rs 25,000
Minimum monthly income for business loans: Rs 30,000
For co-applicant loans, combined income is considered with 80% of secondary income.

2.2 Acceptable Income Proof
Salaried: Last 3 months salary slips, Form 16, 6 months bank statement.
Self-employed: Last 2 years ITR with computation, CA certified financials.
Salary must be credited to bank account — cash salary is not acceptable.
Salary slip must show employer name, employee name, PAN, gross, and net salary.

2.3 Income Stability Assessment
Irregular income patterns in the last 6 months may require additional justification.
Variable pay components above 30% of CTC require conservative assessment.
Bonus and incentives are excluded from primary income calculation.

3. LOAN AMOUNT AND TENURE

3.1 Maximum Loan Limits
Personal loans: Maximum Rs 50,00,000 (50 lakhs)
Home loans: Maximum Rs 5,00,00,000 (5 crores)
Vehicle loans: Maximum Rs 75,00,000 (75 lakhs)
Education loans: Maximum Rs 25,00,000 (25 lakhs) for domestic, Rs 75,00,000 for abroad.

3.2 Loan-to-Income Ratio
Loan amount must not exceed 60 times monthly income for personal loans.
For home loans, EMI must not exceed 40% of net monthly income.
Existing loan obligations are deducted before computing eligibility.

3.3 Tenure Guidelines
Personal loans: 1 to 7 years
Home loans: 5 to 30 years (subject to age at maturity)
Vehicle loans: 1 to 7 years
Business loans: 1 to 10 years

4. DEBT BURDEN ASSESSMENT

4.1 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
Maximum FOIR including new loan EMI must not exceed 50% of gross monthly income.
For applicants with income above Rs 1 lakh/month, FOIR limit is relaxed to 55%.
Government employees are allowed up to 60% FOIR.
FOIR calculation must include all EMIs, rent obligations, and credit card minimum dues.

4.2 Existing Loan Assessment
Maximum 3 active loans permissible for new loan sanction.
Applicants with more than 3 active loans require senior underwriter approval.
Home loans are excluded from the active loan count for FOIR purposes.

4.3 Debt-to-Income Ratio
Total outstanding debt (all loans combined) should not exceed 12 times annual income.
High debt levels require detailed financial assessment and may trigger manual review.

5. CREDIT HISTORY AND BUREAU ASSESSMENT

5.1 Minimum Credit Score Requirements
Standard loans: Minimum CIBIL score of 650
Premium/unsecured loans: Minimum CIBIL score of 700
Home loans: Minimum CIBIL score of 650
Applicants without credit history (NTC — New To Credit): Treated as 650 score with additional documentation.

5.2 Credit Report Analysis
Any default in the last 24 months is grounds for automatic rejection.
Settlements within 36 months require detailed explanation and senior approval.
Credit inquiries above 6 in last 6 months indicate credit-seeking behavior and trigger review.
DPD (Days Past Due) above 90 in last 12 months leads to rejection.

5.3 Bureau Exceptions
Applicants with CIBIL scores between 600-649 may be considered for secured loans only.
Government employees with scores 600-649 may be considered with salary account relationship.

6. RISK CLASSIFICATION AND PRICING

6.1 Risk Bands
Low Risk (credit score 750+, FOIR < 35%, stable employment): Standard interest rate, fast-track processing.
Medium Risk (credit score 650-749, FOIR 35-45%): Standard processing with full documentation.
High Risk (credit score 600-649, FOIR 45-50%): Senior underwriter review mandatory, higher rate.
Very High Risk (credit score below 600, FOIR > 50%): Reject or secured loan only.

6.2 High-Risk Applicant Policy
High-risk applicants are restricted to loan amounts below Rs 10,00,000 (10 lakhs).
High-risk applicants require 2 guarantors or collateral equal to loan amount.
Interest rate premium of 2-4% is applied to high-risk profiles.

6.3 Fraud Indicators
Any discrepancy between OCR-extracted document data and form data triggers fraud review.
Income on salary slip greater than 30% above stated income triggers verification.
PAN card on blacklist or under fraud watch results in immediate rejection.
Multiple applications within 30 days from same PAN triggers review.

7. DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS

7.1 Mandatory Documents for All Loans
Original PAN card (front and back photograph)
Aadhaar card for address verification
Last 3 months salary slips or 2 years ITR
6 months bank statement from primary salary account
Passport size photograph
Loan application form with self-declaration

7.2 Document Verification Standards
All documents must be legible — blurred or low-quality images may be rejected.
PAN number extracted via OCR must match stated PAN on application form.
Salary amount on slip must be within 20% of stated monthly income.
Employer name on salary slip must match employer details on application.

7.3 Additional Documents for High-Value Loans
Loans above Rs 25 lakhs: Form 26AS, property documents if applicable.
Loans above Rs 50 lakhs: CA-certified net worth statement.
Self-employed loans above Rs 10 lakhs: GST registration and 2 years financial statements.

8. SPECIAL CATEGORIES

8.1 Self-Employed Assessment
Business vintage of minimum 2 years required.
Business profitability demonstrated through ITR for 2 consecutive years.
Business bank account statements for 12 months required.
Self-employed professionals (doctors, CAs, architects) have relaxed documentation norms.

8.2 Women Applicants
Women applicants with independent income receive 0.25% interest rate concession.
Priority processing for women-owned business loan applications.

8.3 Senior Citizens
Applicants above 60 years: maximum tenure restricted to 10 years.
Pension income acceptable as primary income for senior citizens.
Family member as co-applicant recommended for applicants above 60.

9. DECISION AUTHORITY

9.1 Auto-Approval Criteria
Applications meeting ALL criteria (credit score 700+, FOIR <40%, no rule failures, verified documents) may be auto-approved up to Rs 5 lakhs.

9.2 Manual Review Triggers
Credit score between 650-699 with loan amount above Rs 10 lakhs.
Any document discrepancy flagged by OCR verification.
FOIR between 45-50%.
Self-employed applicants with business vintage under 3 years.
Applications from applicants with 2+ existing loans.

9.3 Senior Underwriter Approval Required
Any exception to standard eligibility criteria.
Loan amounts above Rs 50 lakhs.
High-risk applicants with compensating factors.
All applications where AI recommendation conflicts with bureau data.

10. REJECTION AND COMMUNICATION

10.1 Grounds for Automatic Rejection
PAN on blacklist or under fraud watch.
Age outside 21-65 band.
Credit score below 600.
FOIR above 60% even after co-applicant addition.
Active default on any existing loan.
Fraudulent or tampered document detection.

10.2 Communication of Decision
Approved: Sanction letter issued within 2 business days.
Rejected: Rejection letter with specific reason codes issued within 3 business days.
Manual Review: Acknowledgment within 1 business day, decision within 7 business days.
Applicants have right to appeal rejection within 30 days with additional documentation.
